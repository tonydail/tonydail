About Me

I specialize in guiding cross-functional teams, designing scalable architectures, and delivering high-quality solutions across mobile, web, API, and cloud platforms.

Professional Focus

My expertise spans the full technology stack with a focus on backend systems, but I'm equally comfortable working across mobile and web platforms. I bring deep knowledge in SQL, JavaScript, HTML, CSS, .NET, along with modern frameworks and tools like Docker, Node.js, PostgreSQL, MS SQL Server, Azure, and GitHub. What sets me apart is my ability to bridge technical expertise with Scrum leadership and stakeholder management. I thrive in collaborative, agile environments where I can mentor teams, align cross-functional groups, and ensure we're delivering software that truly solves user problems.
